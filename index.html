📖 How to Use

📌 Browsing & Boards
  • Board Directory: Select a board above to browse active discussions.
  • Live Auto-Update: New posts appear automatically every 15 seconds without losing your place.
  • Archive: Click [📦 Archive] on any board to read discussions idle for over 3 days.
💬 Posting & Media Formatting
  • Anonymous: Posting is 100% anonymous by default.
  • Greentext: Start any line with > (e.g. >tfw my oshi is streaming).
  • Quoting: Click any No.xxxx to quote a post or reply.
  • Auto-Links: Web URLs (http/https) are auto-linkified.
  • Media: Click Upload Image for instant free hosting, or paste direct YouTube/X (Twitter) links.
🔔 Cross-Device (You) & Replies
  • (You) Sync: Log in on your phone and PC—all your authored threads and replies keep their (You) tags across devices!
  • Reply Counter: A red badge [🔔 Replies] alerts you whenever someone quotes or replies to your post.
⭐ Thread Watchlist
  • Click [⭐ Watch] on any thread to follow it.
  • Open [⭐ Watchlist] in the top bar to track reply counts, bumps, and quickly jump into your discussions.
🛡️ Accounts & Verified Badges
  • Optional: You don't need an account to read or post.
  • Anti-Impersonation: Registering reserves your handle. Verified VTubers and staff can display official badges (e.g. Verified Talent ⭐).

📜 OshiMY Guidelines

1. No illegal content (CSAM). 2. No spam or commercial advertising. 3. Be civil regarding local talents and their identities. 4. Post in the appropriate boards.
